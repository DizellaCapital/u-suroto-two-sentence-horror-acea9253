# Two-Sentence Horror

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253` (Two-Sentence Horror, pot $2, winner-takes-all).

The entry is a horror story of exactly two sentences (232 characters): a dog that barks at the empty armchair by the window every evening at six — and keeps barking at the empty floor after the chair is hauled to the garage. The second sentence reframes the first: he was never barking at the chair.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the story to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), validates it (exactly two sentences, 1–400 characters, not only whitespace), and verifies it by read-back.
